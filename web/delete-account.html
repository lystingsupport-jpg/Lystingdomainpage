LysTing · Privacy Policy · Support

Delete your LysTing account

You can request deletion of a LysTing customer or service-provider account without installing the app.

Request deletion

Email lysting.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my LysTing account. Include your account role and registered contact identifier. Do not send passcodes, identity documents or payment details by email.

What happens next

LysTing Support verifies account ownership before processing. If you can sign in, you can also start a deletion request from your account profile. We revoke active sessions and delete or anonymise account information and related records where applicable. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, legal obligations, fraud prevention, dispute handling or records shared with other users.

Backups

Encrypted backup archives are retained for up to seven days and removed on their normal retention schedule. A deletion request does not restore an account from a backup.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or registered contact method, explain that in your email and Support will explain verification options.
