Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

LysTing connects customers with local service providers. Contact us at lysting.support@gmail.com.

Information we handle

We handle account and contact details, authentication records, profile information, service listings, service requests, booking and status records, messages, reviews, support tickets and media users choose to upload. Providers may also provide business and verification information for provider onboarding.

How we use it

We use information to provide the app, authenticate accounts, connect customers and providers, provide support, moderate content, prevent abuse and protect service security. We do not sell personal information.

Location and permissions

When you choose location features, the app may send location to help identify an area. The service stores resulting area information needed for the feature rather than raw coordinates. You can decline location, camera or media permissions and manage them in device settings.

Sharing

Information needed for a service interaction is shared with the other party. We use providers that host our application, database, storage and support systems. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention and security

We keep information while an account is active and afterwards only as needed for support, security, legal obligations, dispute handling or legitimate operational purposes. Encrypted backup archives are retained for up to seven days.

Your choices

You can update account information in the app, report objectionable content, block chat contacts, contact support or request deletion. See Delete your account.

Changes

We may update this policy when services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published here.
